Overwatch HUD

Series

Won the map gives the map being played to that team (its maps won go up on the overlay and the map bans). A drawn map adds a map to the series. New series clears the scores and the map bans.

Post-game stats

Drop a scoreboard screenshot here, paste one (Ctrl+V) or click to choose one: the players' names and E / A / D / DMG / H are read from it. Check them, and pick the heroes.

Type them in from the end-of-map scoreboard (E = eliminations, A = assists, D = deaths, DMG = damage, H = healing). They go to the GFX Engine's Team stats (DMG, Kills, Deaths) and Player stats (+ Healing), with each player's hero when the engine's player pictures are set to champions / agents / heroes. Use Global Information on the GFX Engine page fills in the players from the engine's teams.

Overlay

Teams

The record is the team's season record beside its name (e.g. 3-1). Drop a logo on its box, or click to choose. GFX Engine's Use Global Information brings the teams' names, colours and logos.

Tournament

The logo is in the middle of SCC 3 and at the top of the map bans.

Map bans

Fill it in as the teams choose: the graphic shows it live, on air too. Picked maps go into the series (and up on the overlay as the map being played); who won each map is set here or on the Match page.
